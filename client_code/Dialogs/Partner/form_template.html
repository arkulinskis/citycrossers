🏷️ Become a partner stop

Partner places get a deal badge on the CityCrossers map, appear in the Perks page, and are suggested to hosts planning routes nearby.

  • 1️⃣Pick an offer walkers will love: a discount, a free refill, a group table.
  • 2️⃣Our team adds your place and deal to the map (admins do this from the Admin page).
  • 3️⃣Hungry groups arrive. You get reviews from people who walked 12 km to reach you.

The partner programme is opening city by city. Create an account and say hello in your profile bio with your café's name, and an admin will reach out.
